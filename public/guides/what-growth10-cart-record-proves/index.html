What a GROWTH10 Cart Record Proves

How to read a dated cart observation without overstating what happened at checkout.

Short answer: a GROWTH10 cart record proves what was visibly present in the anonymous cart at the recorded time: the product, subtotal, code reduction, displayed total, and whether a payment method was selected.

Discount-code pages often flatten several different questions into one label: Was the code entered? Did the cart show a reduction? Was payment attempted? Was an order completed? A careful GROWTH10 record answers only the questions supported by the retained cart evidence.

Why the subtotal and total are different questions

The code reduction is evaluated against the eligible cart subtotal. The displayed total is the final visible cart figure after the other lines currently shown by the merchant. Because a cart can contain shipping, tax, promotional, or payment-method-related adjustments, readers should not describe the entire total difference as the GROWTH10 discount.

Plain-language interpretation: the record supports “GROWTH10 showed a CAD $7.00 reduction in this cart observation.” It does not support “an order was completed” or “the same result is guaranteed for every cart.”

What “owner-reviewed” means

Owner-reviewed identifies the provenance of this published observation: the original cart capture was supplied and reviewed for the site-specific record. It does not turn the observation into a purchase receipt, and it does not remove the distinction between a cart result and a merchant order confirmation.

FAQ

Did GROWTH10 require a payment?

No. The observation stopped before payment, identity, address, order submission, and purchase completion.

What does the code reduction show?

It shows the amount visibly attributed to GROWTH10 in that cart state: CAD $7.00 against a CAD $70.00 subtotal.

Can the result change?

Yes. Product eligibility, merchant promotions, shipping, payment-method incentives, and other cart conditions can change. The record is dated so readers can understand its scope.
