What the screenshots establish

The first two captures depict the same cart, comprising eight units across two merchandise lines. The order summary identifies a coupon reduction and a line labelled “Bitcoin Discount (20%).” The third capture shows a separate three-unit cart with a line labelled “Interac e-Transfer discount (10%).” In both cases, the merchant displayed shipping as calculated at checkout.

The original merchandise values are calculated from the crossed-out unit prices and quantities visible in the supplied captures. The displayed payment reductions were based on the remaining subtotal after the coupon reduction, rather than on the original merchandise value. The calculated overall percentages are therefore sequential reductions, not the sum of the individual advertised percentages.

Evidence and limitations

Source: three owner-supplied mobile cart screenshots. Two depict the Bitcoin-labelled example, and one depicts the Interac-labelled example. The captures do not independently establish a calendar date, an exact promotion cutoff, final shipping or taxes, payment completion, or availability across other baskets. The editorial preparation date is not a verification timestamp.

The stated promotion end date is November 30, 2026, as supplied by the site owner. The exact cutoff time and time zone have not been independently established. This article remains a historical analysis and should not be interpreted as a live offer or product endorsement.
